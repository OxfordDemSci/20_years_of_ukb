The growth analysis used the Dimensions all-endpoints-wide UK Biobank publication parquet with a data cutoff of 2026-08-26. Publication trends were restricted to complete calendar years 2013–2025; provisional 2026 records were retained only for data-quality auditing. Publications were deduplicated by Dimensions ID. Clinical trials, patents, policy documents, grants and datasets were reconstructed from paired endpoint ID and event-date arrays and deduplicated by endpoint ID; IDs without corresponding dates were retained with a missing event year. In the primary figure, a publication carried an endpoint indicator when at least one linked endpoint ID was present; it contributed once per indicator, and indicators were allowed to overlap. Conditional co-occurrence in Figure 1D was the pairwise intersection divided by the number of publications carrying the row indicator; diagonal self-comparisons were omitted. Unique endpoint tables assigned each endpoint to the publication year of its earliest linked UK Biobank paper. Cross-sector collaboration required both an Education organization and at least one Company, Government, Healthcare or Nonprofit organization. Sector-specific cross-sector categories were not mutually exclusive. International collaboration required at least two research-organization countries. Open-access papers contained oa_all in the source access array, and preprints had Dimensions publication type preprint. Altmetric and citation measures represent the current values stored in the parquet and were grouped by publication year; the data did not contain news-specific mention counts. Supplementary pairwise overlap used the Jaccard intersection-over-union coefficient; the symmetric upper triangle and diagonal were omitted from the display. Linked-endpoint prevalence for recent publication cohorts may be affected by indexing and linkage latency.
