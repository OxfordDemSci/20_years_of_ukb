Supplementary Figure 2 | Name-inferred gender composition of UK Biobank authorships. (A) Annual author-paper counts assigned Female, Male or Unknown/androgynous. (B) Annual classification coverage under strict dictionary, expanded dictionary, five-library offline ensemble and primary identity-linked rules. The ensemble uses only package-bundled local data with explicit disagreement thresholds; the primary rule adds conflict-safe linkage across records carrying the same Dimensions researcher ID. (C) Female-name share among classified names by authorship position, with 95% Wilson intervals. Corresponding authorship can overlap positional roles. (D) Female-name share in the 12 FOR divisions with the most classified author-paper memberships. Panel D uses a sequential blue scale for legibility, not a second variable. These binary categories are probabilistic proxies from names, not observed sex or self-identified gender; Unknown is retained because both error and non-classification vary culturally.
