Supplementary Figure 1 | UK Biobank-specific author productivity and impact. (A) Complementary cumulative distribution of publication counts, annotated with the median, interquartile range, upper-tail share and maximum. (B) Distribution of UKB h-index values, truncated at the 99.5th percentile for legibility and annotated with distributional summaries. (C) Association between UKB publication count and UKB h-index, with the Spearman rank correlation reported. (D) Authors with the ten highest UKB h-index values, with publication and citation counts. Citation measures are snapshot values and the UKB h-index uses only publications in the analysed corpus.
